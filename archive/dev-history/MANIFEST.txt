IIP D-OBSIDIAN-06 Compatibility Fix
=========================================

Purpose
-------
Restore the original KnowledgeEventAdapter persistence contract after the
Obsidian projection integration introduced direct calls to sync_*_projection()
methods on bridge test doubles.

Change
------
event_adapter.py no longer requires:
- sync_decision_projection
- sync_snapshot_projection
- sync_evidence_projection

The adapter persists the Knowledge model and returns it, preserving backward
compatibility. Obsidian projection/synchronization remains a Bridge/service
responsibility and should be tested at that integration boundary.
